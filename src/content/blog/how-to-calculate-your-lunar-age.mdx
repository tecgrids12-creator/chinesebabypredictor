The Chinese gender chart doesn't run on your birth certificate — it runs on the traditional Chinese counting system called **virtual age (虚岁, xūsuì)**. Here's the whole math in four steps.

## Rule 1: you are born age 1

In China's traditional counting, the time in the womb counts as life. You arrive **age 1**, not 0.

## Rule 2: everyone gains a year at Chinese New Year

Aging happens not on your birthday but at **Spring Festival (Chinese New Year)**. So:

**Lunar age = (lunar year at the moment — lunar year of birth) + 1**

## Worked example

- Mother born **January 15, 1989**. The 1989 lunar year started **February 6, 1989** — she was born *before* it, so her lunar birth year is **1988**.
- Conception on **June 5, 2018** → lunar year 2018.
- Lunar age = (2018 − 1988) + 1 = **31**.
- June 5, 2018 was lunar month 4 (April 22, 2018 in lunar terms... at day 22) → lunar month **4**.
- Chart cell: age 31 × month 4 → **girl**, says the [chart](/chinese-gender-chart/).

## Where people slip up

1. **Forgetting the New Year boundary.** Anyone born in January or February needs to check whether CNY had already happened that year.
2. **Using the conception *month* in Gregorian.** Lunar months start on new-moon days, which wander across our months.
3. **Ignoring leap lunar months.** Some years (like 2025) have 13 lunar months.
