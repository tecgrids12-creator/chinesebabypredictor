India is likely the world's biggest audience for playful gender guessing — and precisely where the stakes are most regulated. If you're in India, here's the honest context.

## What the PCPNDT Act says

The **Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques Act (1994)** prohibits prenatal *sex determination* — ultrasound clinics and labs in India are legally barred from revealing a fetus's sex, a law born from historic sex-ratio imbalances. That ban applies to medical practice, and we support it completely.

## What an entertainment predictor like ours is

- A **folklore tool**: it computes one of two outcomes from lunar-calendar arithmetic, the same as a paper chart in any bookstore.
- It **cannot determine sex** — it predicts a coin flip, explicitly labeled for entertainment.
- It makes **no medical claim** and never replaces any test.

That's why you'll find entertainment-only disclaimers on every tool page here rather than tucked in a footer.

## What Indian families actually do

The tradition is alive as a **family game** — grandparents and aunties trade chart predictions, the quiz circulates on WhatsApp, and everyone waits for the [anatomy scan](/blog/when-can-you-find-out-your-baby-s-sex/) like everyone else. Perfectly legal, perfectly wholesome.

## Our commitment

We support gender equality without exceptions. No tool on this site claims medical validity, no result influences care, and we encourage every family — in India and everywhere — to welcome boys and girls equally. 🩵🩷
