Before the ultrasound, families relied on **old wives' tales** to guess boy or girl. Here's the full list of the twenty that refuse to die — plus what research says about each.

## The myths (and the evidence)

1. **Carrying low = boy.** Belly shape depends on muscle tone and baby's position — not sex.
2. **Salty cravings = boy, sweet = girl.** Craving studies find no link.
3. **Severe morning sickness = girl.** Slight statistical tilt toward girls with hyperemesis, but far too weak for prediction.
4. **Faster fetal heart rate (>140) = girl.** Reviewed thoroughly — no correlation after early pregnancy.
5. **Glowing skin = boy, breakouts = girl ("daughters steal your beauty").** No evidence.
6. **Ring test: circles = girl, pendulum swings = boy.** Random motion, famously unreliable.
7. **Left breast bigger = girl.** Anecdote only.
8. **Mood swings = girl.** Hormones don't discriminate by sex.
9. **Dad isn't gaining weight "sympathy pregnancy" = boy.** Cute, untested, unknowable.
10. **Cold feet = boy.** Circulation, not chromosomes.
11. **Dry hands = boy, soft hands = girl.** Pure folklore.
12. **Sleeping on your left side = girl.** Position changes nightly.
13. **Chinese chart = 50/50.** Tested — chance.
14. **Mayan parity = 50/50.** By construction.
15. **Heartburn = hairy baby (not sex, just fun).** One small study *did* weakly correlate heartburn with newborn hair!
16. **Morning sickness stops at 12 weeks exactly.** Varies for everyone.
17. **Curly hair line test on the belly = girl.** Folklore.
18. **First trimester blood draw "tells everything" (NIPT).** Actually true — this one's real medicine.
19. **Baking soda fizz test.** Home chemistry theater.
20. **The "conception dreams" of snakes or fish.** Charming, unverifiable.
