Planning a 2027 baby? 2027 is the **Year of the Fire Goat (未羊)**; its lunar year begins **February 6, 2027**. As always, two things change each calendar year and one thing never does.

## What changes for 2027

- **Chinese New Year boundary:** Feb 6, 2027. Conceptions before that date belong to the 2026 Fire Horse lunar year.
- **Month mapping:** each lunar month starts on slightly different Gregorian dates — e.g., early January 2027 belongs to lunar month 12 of the Horse year.
- **Your lunar age row:** mothers born just before a Chinese New Year shift rows later in the chart for conceptions early in the year.

## What never changes

The chart grid itself — 28 ages × 12 months, the same cells every year. The "2027 predictor" is just the same chart with 2027's conversion applied.
