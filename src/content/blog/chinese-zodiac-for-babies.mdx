Unlike the [gender chart](/chinese-gender-chart/), the Chinese zodiac is a genuine calendar tradition with deep cultural resonance. Whichever year your baby arrives, they'll carry a sign — the legend says of it also governs the personality. Here's the whole menagerie.

| Year | Sign | The story says… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2024 🐉 | Wood Dragon | Confident, charismatic, born leaders |
| 2025 🐍 | Wood Snake | Wise, intuitive, graceful under pressure |
| 2026 🐎 | Fire Horse | Bold, energetic, fiercely independent |
| 2027 🐐 | Fire Goat | Gentle, creative, empathetic |
| 2028 🐵 | Earth Monkey | Clever, adaptable, endlessly curious |

**The 12 animals in order:** Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig — each paired with one of five elements in a 60-year cycle.

## Two signs for one pregnancy?

If your [due date](/due-date-calculator/) sits near **Chinese New Year**, your baby could plausibly arrive in either zodiac year — early 2026 babies are Snake-or-Horse, for example. Try imagining both personalities for the nursery name list; it's a quirky joy most parents miss.

## The gender chart doesn't use the zodiac (important!)

Popular misconception: the zodiac animal does **not** change chart predictions. The chart runs purely on **lunar age × lunar month**; the Fire Horse affects nothing but birthdays and horoscopes. Anyone selling you a "zodiac-corrected chart" is inventing something new.
