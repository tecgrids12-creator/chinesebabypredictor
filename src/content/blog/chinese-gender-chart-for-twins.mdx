Twins complicate *everything* — including the gender chart. Here's the honest breakdown.

## What the chart is built for

The [Chinese gender chart](/chinese-gender-chart/) returns **one cell — one boy/girl verdict** from a mother's lunar age and lunar month of conception. It was never designed with multiples in mind, and no official leap or twin variant exists in the traditional sources.

## The three twin scenarios

- **Boy + girl twins:** the single chart cell can, at best, describe one of them — the other flips the prediction by definition.
- **Two boys or two girls:** the cell "matches" both by luck half the time.
- **Fraternals vs. identicals:** identical twins are always same-sex (one egg); fraternal twins can differ. The chart has no concept of any of this.

## Fun workaround families still use

Many twin-expecting families run the chart per baby by pretending each was conceived on the **estimated conception day of each placenta's implantation estimate** — which is medically unknowable in utero. In other words: pure theater, enjoyed anyway. 🎭

## Knowing real answers for twins

- **NIPT works normally with twins** and can report whether both are boys, both girls, or mixed (with slightly reduced confidence when results differ).
- **The anatomy ultrasound** is usually able to sex both babies independently at 18–20 weeks.
