Chinese New Year 2026 falls on **February 17**, which makes 2026 the Year of the **Fire Horse** 🐎 — and the year in which searches for the *Chinese gender calendar 2026* go through the roof. Here's everything you need, in one place.

## What the 2026 calendar is (and isn't)

The Chinese gender calendar — also called the Chinese birth chart or **Qing Palace Chart (清宫图)** — is a grid of the mother's **lunar age** (18–45) against the **lunar month of conception** (1–12). Every cell says either boy or girl.

The crucial thing to understand: **there is exactly one authentic chart, and it never changes from year to year.** A "2026 edition" is the same grid — what *is* new in 2026 is the mapping:

- **Chinese New Year lands on February 17**, so lunar ages "reset" later than in 2025.
- Lunar months map onto different Gregorian dates every year.
- The zodiac animal (Fire Horse) is cultural symbolism — it does **not** change any cell.

## How to use it for a 2026 pregnancy

1. **Convert the mother's birth date to lunar.** A mom born March 4, 1995 has lunar birth year 1995; one born January 10, 1995 (before the Feb 10, 1995 new year) actually counts as lunar 1994 — which changes her lunar age.
2. **Convert the conception date to the lunar month.** For early March 2026, the lunar year is 2025 until Feb 17, 2026; from Feb 17 it's lunar year 2026 (the Fire Horse).
3. **Look up the intersection.** Lunar age × lunar month → boy or girl.

## How accurate is the 2026 chart?

Circumstantially identical to every other year: **about 50%, the same as chance**. A University of Michigan analysis found no better-than-random alignment, and the oft-cited "93% accurate" number has no scientific source. Fun? Absolutely. A pregnancy test? No.

## Common 2026 mistakes

- **Reading the chart with Gregorian dates.** January 2026 conceptions belong to lunar year 2025 and often lunar month 11 or 12 — not lunar month 1.
- **Ignoring the leap month.** The 2025 lunar year (through Feb 16, 2026) includes a leap 6th month, so late-summer 2025 conceptions can straddle it.
- **Using Western age.** The chart expects lunar age — usually 1–2 years older.
