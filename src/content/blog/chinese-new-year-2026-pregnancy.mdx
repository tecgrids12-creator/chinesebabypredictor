## February 17, 2026 — the boundary day

Chinese New Year 2026 begins the **Year of the Horse**, specifically the **Fire Horse (丙午)**, a combo returning every 60 years. Two pregnancy-relevant boundaries move that day:

1. **Lunar ages reset** — everyone gains a virtual year (age +1), which changes chart rows for conceptions after Feb 17.
2. **Lunar month 1 begins** — conceptions before mid-February 2026 were mapped to the *previous* Snake year's months.

## What's a Fire Horse baby like, culturally?

Fire Horse years are storied: **energetic, bold, charismatic, independent** — with a folkloric reputation for strong will. The 1966 Fire Horse year was famously intense in parts of East Asia, where superstition briefly tanked birth rates. Modern folklore embracing has been kinder: a Fire Horse child is framed as a natural-born leader. 🐎🔥

## Zodies for 2026 babies

- Born **before Feb 17, 2026** → **Snake** 🐍 (intuitive, graceful)
- Born **Feb 17, 2026 or after** → **Horse** 🐎 (free-spirited, energetic)
