While folklore tools can't beat a coin flip, medicine can. Here's exactly **when** each reliable method becomes possible.

## NIPT — from about 10 weeks

**Noninvasive prenatal testing** analyzes fragments of fetal DNA from a standard maternal blood draw. It screens for chromosomal conditions and — in most systems — reports fetal sex with **very high** reliability as early as ten weeks. Results typically take one to two weeks; NIPT is a screening test, so confirm any concerning result with diagnostic testing.

## Anatomy ultrasound — 18 to 20 weeks

The famous "20-week scan" (technically 18–22 weeks for most) evaluates development, and sex can usually be determined when the baby cooperates — position matters! Timing, fetal position and sonographer practice all affect visibility.

## Amniocentesis and CVS

Reserved for medical indications, these diagnostic tests reveal chromosomes definitively — but no responsible provider performs them just to learn sex.

## What the calendar methods can't do

The [Chinese gender chart](/chinese-gender-chart/) and its cousins find no scientific validation: University of Michigan testing found chance-level results. Use them for [fun](/chinese-gender-predictor/) — never for decisions.
