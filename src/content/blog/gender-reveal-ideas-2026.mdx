You've tried the [Chinese gender predictor](/chinese-gender-predictor/), polled the [Mayan calendar](/mayan-gender-predictor/), balanced the [ring test](/gender-quiz/)… and your [anatomy scan](/blog/when-can-you-find-out-your-baby-s-sex/) is weeks away. Or you already know and want the party. Either way — here are twelve reveal formats we adore.

## Low budget, high drama

1. **Balloon pop** — one black balloon, pink or blue confetti inside. Cost: a few dollars. Photos: chef's kiss.
2. **Silhouette cake** — white cake with a colored center, cut on camera. Classic for a reason.
3. **Scratch-off cards** for the whole family (printable templates everywhere).
4. **Colored smoke arc** for outdoors — dramatic, still affordable.

## Family-can-fly-in-level

5. **Pinata of tiny bottles** — guests grab one; colors reveal the story.
6. **Guess-the-gender wall** — chart your whole family's folklore predictions next to the [chart](/chinese-gender-chart/).
7. **"Boy or girl" clue trail** ending at a decorated nursery closet.
8. **Painted handprints** — baby's gender-colored paint on a canvas everyone signs.

## The instantly shareable

9. **Confetti cannon at golden hour** — one take, one clip, endless reposts.
10. **Pet announces** — dog's bandana or cat's collar take center stage.
11. **Drone shot** spelling it in a field or on a beach.
12. **The Chinese Baby Predictor screenshot reveal** — your lunar conversion result shared the moment you guessed. (Okay, we're biased. 🥰)

**Pro tip:** schedule the party *after* your NIPT or scan, and let the folklore tools be the *pre-game* the family argues about at the table — not the reveal itself.
