Our [gender planning calendar](/gender-planner/) reads the chart **backwards**: pick a desired gender and it lists the lunar months whose cells match. It's a stretch of the tradition that countless families love — but let's be transparent about the two layers here.

## Layer 1: the tradition

The Qing Palace chart exists, faithfully, as a fixed grid. Reading it backwards is arithmetically trivial: for your lunar age, some months say boy and some say girl. Our planner does the lunar math precisely and gives you **exact Gregorian windows** ("conceive between Mar 19 and Apr 16") rather than vague "month #5" instructions.

## Layer 2: the science

Real sex-ratio *swaying* claims have come and gone — the Shettles timing method, diets high in potassium, pH theories. The strongest systematic reviews find:

- **Timing intercourse barely moves the needle** and Shettles' method has been repeatedly unsupported when tested properly.
- **Maternal diet before conception** shows some weak observational hints (more total calories ~ male-biased odds) but nothing reproducible or clinically actionable.
- **The only effective "methods"** are IVF with preimplantation genetic testing (PGT) — illegal in many countries and ethically restricted precisely because sex should not be a selection criterion.

## How to enjoy the planner responsibly

1. Compute your [lunar age](/lunar-age-calculator/).
2. Open the [planner](/gender-planner/) and see the chart's "best months."
3. Try to conceive naturally whenever ovulation says go — the calendar can be your ritual, **not** your reason.
4. Celebrate whatever appears! 💙💗
