Chinese Gender Predictor 2027: Year of the Fire Goat Chart & Planner

February 15, 2026 · Chinese Baby Predictor Team

Planning a 2027 baby? 2027 is the Year of the Fire Goat (未羊); its lunar year begins February 6, 2027. As always, two things change each calendar year and one thing never does.

What changes for 2027

  • Chinese New Year boundary: Feb 6, 2027. Conceptions before that date belong to the 2026 Fire Horse lunar year.
  • Month mapping: each lunar month starts on slightly different Gregorian dates — e.g., early January 2027 belongs to lunar month 12 of the Horse year.
  • Your lunar age row: mothers born just before a Chinese New Year shift rows later in the chart for conceptions early in the year.

What never changes

The chart grid itself — 28 ages × 12 months, the same cells every year. The “2027 predictor” is just the same chart with 2027’s conversion applied.

Using our tools for 2027 conceptions

  1. Enter your dates — the predictor supports any conception between 1940 and today, 2027 conceptions included.
  2. Open the planning calendar and choose 2027 to see the exact Gregorian windows that match your desired gender.
  3. Cross-check your converted dates on the interactive chart.

Fire Goat babies

Goats in Chinese culture are gentle, creative souls — Fire adds charisma and drive. Bonus: many parents in the region historically preferred this pairing, so a 2027 baby is a lucky charm before it’s even born. 🐐🔥

As always: fun first, NIPT/ultrasound for certainty.
