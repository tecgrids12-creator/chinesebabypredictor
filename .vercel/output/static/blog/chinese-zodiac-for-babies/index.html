Chinese Zodiac for Babies: What Your Child’s Year Sign Means

February 27, 2026 · Chinese Baby Predictor Team

Unlike the gender chart, the Chinese zodiac is a genuine calendar tradition with deep cultural resonance. Whichever year your baby arrives, they’ll carry a sign — the legend says of it also governs the personality. Here’s the whole menagerie.

YearSignThe story says…
2024 🐉Wood DragonConfident, charismatic, born leaders
2025 🐍Wood SnakeWise, intuitive, graceful under pressure
2026 🐎Fire HorseBold, energetic, fiercely independent
2027 🐐Fire GoatGentle, creative, empathetic
2028 🐵Earth MonkeyClever, adaptable, endlessly curious

The 12 animals in order: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig — each paired with one of five elements in a 60-year cycle.

Two signs for one pregnancy?

If your due date sits near Chinese New Year, your baby could plausibly arrive in either zodiac year — early 2026 babies are Snake-or-Horse, for example. Try imagining both personalities for the nursery name list; it’s a quirky joy most parents miss.

The gender chart doesn’t use the zodiac (important!)

Popular misconception: the zodiac animal does not change chart predictions. The chart runs purely on lunar age × lunar month; the Fire Horse affects nothing but birthdays and horoscopes. Anyone selling you a “zodiac-corrected chart” is inventing something new.
