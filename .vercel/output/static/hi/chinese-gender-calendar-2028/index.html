Chinese New Year 2028: January 26, 2028

The Year of the Earth Monkey (戊申) starts unusually early. Because the lunar year starts on Jan 26, 2028, virtually all of Gregorian 2028 sits inside the Monkey lunar year — the crossover trap seen in other years is confined to the first 25 days of 2028 (Fire Goat lunar months).

🌙

The 2028 Intercalary Leap 5th Month (*Runyue)

Lunar year 2028 carries 13 months; the extra month follows month 5: Jun 23, 2028 – Jul 21, 2028. The traditional half-month rule reads days 1–15 as month 5 and days 16–29 as month 6 — so summer 2028 conceptions have two valid chart columns depending on their date:

Days 1–15 → M5 (Jun 23 – Jul 7) Days 16–29 → M6 (Jul 8 – Jul 21)

2028 calendar FAQ

When is Chinese New Year 2028 and what is special about that year?

+

Chinese New Year 2028 falls on January 26, 2028, opening the Year of the Earth Monkey (戊申). Because the lunar year starts so early, virtually all of the 2028 Gregorian year sits inside the Monkey lunar year.

What is the 2028 leap 5th month (Runyue) and how is it read?

+

Lunar year 2028 (Earth Monkey) carries an intercalary 5th month: 13 months total. Traditional practice splits the leap month in half — days 1–15 count as month 5, days 16–29 as month 6. A conception in the first half reads month 5 in the chart, a conception in the second half reads month 6. Our table maps both halves to exact Gregorian dates.

Why does a 13-month lunar year matter for the gender chart?

+

The chart only has columns for months 1–12, so the extra leap month must be folded back into a numbered month using the half-month rule. Simple calculators either skip it or mislabel it, producing contradictory results — especially for June–July 2028 conceptions, where the leap month lands.

How old is a 1990-born mother in the 2028 chart?

+

Under the virtual-age rule she is lunar 39 during the 2028 Monkey year (39 = 2028 − 1990 + 1), assuming her birthday falls after the 1990 CNY cutoff; born before the cutoff she counts from lunar 1990 and is lunar 40. Either number selects her chart row — the planner computes it exactly from her date of birth.
