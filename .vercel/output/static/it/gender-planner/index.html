How to read your planner results

The planner walks through both lunar years that overlap your chosen Gregorian year, computes your lunar age for each, and keeps only the months whose chart cell matches your wish. For every match you get the exact Gregorian window (for example: "Sep 22 – Oct 20, 2026") plus month numbers and leap-month notes — the clarity vague "year of the elephant" tables can't give you.

Tips for timing cycles with windows

Conception happens near ovulation — about 14 days after a period begins. If a window opens mid-month, check where your own ovulation typically falls relative to it. If ovulation lands outside every happy window this year… welcome to the entire history of folklore family planning: nature keeps her own calendar.
