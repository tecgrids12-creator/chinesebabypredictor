Understanding Sperm Genetics: Why Every Conception is 50/50

A baby's chromosomal sex is decided the instant one sperm meets the egg — and by biology, not calendars. Every sperm carries either an X or a Y chromosome, produced in roughly equal numbers, so each conception begins at approximately even odds no matter what the lunar month says.

The chart's observed performance confirms this: the largest empirical test yet — a population-registry study of Sweden's 2.8 million births (Villamor et al., 2010) — found the traditional lunar method matched natural variation at about 50.0%, exactly the coin-flip rate chromosomes predict. Use the planner to organise your trying-to-conceive months and savour tradition. For a scientifically certain answer, NIPT (~10 weeks) and the 18–20 week anatomy scan are the only reliable ways to know.

Natural odds worth knowing: worldwide, about 105 boys are born for every 100 girls — a slight natural tilt that no calendar, chart or folklore can move. ✨

Frequently Asked Questions for TTC Parents

Can you use the Chinese gender calendar to plan conceiving a boy or girl?

+

You can use it for lighthearted planning, yes. The chart maps your lunar age and the lunar month of conception to a boy or girl cell, so our TTC planner shows you which upcoming lunar months the tradition marks “boy” or “girl.” There is no scientific evidence the calendar changes outcomes — treat it as fun framing for real conception timing, which is driven by ovulation.

How do I calculate my best conception months for a girl?

+

Enter your date of birth, choose “Baby Girl,” and pick a planning year. The planner converts your birthday into lunar age, walks the lunar months of that year, and lists every window whose Qing Palace chart cell reads girl — each with its exact Gregorian date range, such as “Lunar month 4, June 12 – July 10.” Conception windows at the start of a lunar month leave the most room for the early days of the following month, too.

What is the difference between the Chinese calendar and the Shettles Method for gender swaying?

+

The Chinese calendar is folklore: an astrological lookup table that ignores your cycle and claims roughly 50% accuracy. The Shettles Method is a timing theory: it claims that intercourse 2–4 days before ovulation favours girls and intercourse close to ovulation favours boys, based on X/Y sperm motility differences. Shettles remains scientifically debated (~75% cited by supporters, often criticized), while the calendar’s observational accuracy is a coin flip. Neither replaces medical counselling.

Does timing intercourse with the lunar calendar guarantee baby gender?

+

No. No calendar can guarantee, or even reliably shift, a baby’s sex. Each conception is biologically 50/50 — half of the father’s sperm carry X chromosomes and half carry Y. The lunar planner is a charming way to organize trying-to-conceive months, and only medical procedures (like PGD/IVF embryo screening) can select sex with near-certainty.
